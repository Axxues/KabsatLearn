8-HOUR HACKATHON GUIDELINES

Overview:
The 8-Hour Hackathon aims to challenge participants to develop innovative, technology-enabled, and feasible solutions to identified local problems within a limited period. It seeks to:
Encourage creativity, innovation, and problem-solving using science, technology, and digital solutions;
Develop functional prototypes or proof-of-concepts addressing real community, government, business, or environmental challenges;
Promote the responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), digital technologies, data, and emerging technologies; and
Identify promising solutions with potential for further development, adoption, commercialization, or deployment.

Eligibility and Team Composition:
The hackathon is open to students currently enrolled in college in the Province of La Union and senior high school students of schools in the City of San Fernando, La Union and member schools of the ICT Council.
Each team shall have at most three (3) members and a coach.
Each team shall designate a Team Leader who shall serve as the official representative.
Teams may use AI tools, open-source software, APIs, cloud services, and other legally available digital resources, provided that their use is properly acknowledged and does not violate applicable licenses, copyrights, privacy, or other laws.
Participants under eighteen (18) need signed parent or guardian consent and a school-endorsed adult coach.
All participants must present a valid school ID during the registration proper for identity and eligibility verification.

Challenge:
The Organizers shall announce one or more problem statements/challenge areas before the start of the hackathon. Teams shall select a challenge and develop a technology-based solution within the prescribed eight-hour period.

The proposed solution should demonstrate a clear connection between the problem, proposed intervention, target users, and expected impact.

Hackathon Rules and Mechanics:
The official development period shall be eight (8) hours, excluding orientation and final judging.
Teams must start development only after the official announcement of the challenge.
Teams may use pre-existing libraries, frameworks, APIs, datasets, templates, and development tools. However, the core solution presented must be developed or substantially configured during the hackathon.
Participants may use AI-assisted development tools, including AI coding assistants and generative AI. Teams remain responsible for the accuracy, originality, security, legality, and functionality of their outputs.
Any third-party materials, datasets, APIs, models, or open-source components must be properly acknowledged.
Teams shall not use confidential, proprietary, personal, or illegally obtained data.
Plagiarism, misrepresentation of authorship, unauthorized copying of another team's work, or submission of a previously completed project as the hackathon output shall constitute grounds for disqualification.
Internet access, provided equipment, APIs, datasets, and other resources shall be subject to the facilities and policies established by the Organizers.
Teams must submit their output before the prescribed deadline. Late submissions may be disqualified or penalized at the discretion of the Organizers.
The decision of the Board of Judges shall be final and unappealable.

Required Outputs:
At the end of the eight-hour development period, each team shall submit:
Working Prototype/Demonstration – a functional application, proof-of-concept, mock-up, or demonstrable solution;
Pitch Presentation and Demo – maximum 7 minutes; and
Q&A – maximum 10 minutes, including questions from the judges.

The Organizers may prescribe an online submission repository or standard submission template.

Responsible AI and data rules:
Comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173): no real citizen personal data; use public, anonymized or organizer-provided synthetic data only.
Disclose every AI tool used (LLM APIs, coding assistants, pre-trained models) in the pitch deck.
AI agents and automated features must not auto-approve benefits, diagnose illness or impose penalties; they recommend and an officer decides.
Health solutions must show a clear "not a medical diagnosis" notice and refer to a health worker.
Teams using AI agents must show their prompts in the pitch deck and during Q&A: the system prompt, the tool or function descriptions the agent can call, and 2 to 3 sample conversations with inputs and outputs. 
Store API keys in environment variables, never in public repositories.

Criteria for Judging:
Entries are scored out of 100 points on 4 criteria by a panel of at least three judges: one from the LGU, one AI or ML practitioner, and one domain expert for the track.

Intellectual Property, Data, and Responsible Innovation
The intellectual property rights over the solution shall remain with the participating team, subject to any prior rights, third-party licenses, datasets, or specific agreements with the Organizers, partners, or sponsors.
Participants shall ensure that their outputs comply with applicable Philippine laws on intellectual property, data privacy, cybersecurity, and responsible use of AI and digital technologies.
Teams shall disclose material use of AI-generated content, third-party software, datasets, APIs, or other external resources.
The Organizers may document, photograph, record, and showcase the hackathon outputs for non-commercial promotional, educational, and innovation-development purposes, with proper attribution to the participating teams.
Winning teams may be invited to further develop, pilot, incubate, or present their solutions to relevant government agencies, investors, industry partners, academe, or innovation support organizations.

Disqualification: 
A team may be disqualified for:
Submission after the prescribed deadline;
Plagiarism or unauthorized copying;
Misrepresentation of the team's work or use of a substantially pre-developed solution;
Violation of data privacy, intellectual property, cybersecurity, or applicable laws;
Use of prohibited or illegally obtained resources/data; or
Conduct that materially compromises the integrity and fairness of the competition.
